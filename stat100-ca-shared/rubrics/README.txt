Homework rubrics go here (gradetools format, like ~/project-grading/rubrics):
name, total_points, prompt_code, prompt_message, feedback, points_to_remove
One file per item, e.g. hw04-rubric.csv. Used by the grading tab (phase 2).
Section work is graded on completion (0-100% in steps of 10) and needs no rubric.
